Partner program

Recommend Rewora and earn from it

Do you build stores, run marketing or consult? For every client who starts using Rewora through you, you receive a commission — and the client gets premium care.

Who the program is for

E-commerce agencies

You build client stores on Shoptet, Shopify or from scratch. Reviews are a natural part of the launch — and an extra source of income for you.

Marketing agencies

You look after performance campaigns and SEO. Stars in Google Shopping and review content improve the numbers you report to the client.

Freelancers and consultants

You advise stores on how to grow. Rewora is a tool you can recommend with a clear conscience — and get a share for it.

How it works
01

You get in touch

Send us a few lines about the stores you work with. We will arrange a short call and agree the terms.

02

You get partner access

You get a demo account, materials for presenting to the client and your own link or code so signups are attributed to you.

03

You recommend Rewora to a client

You can roll it out yourself via GTM or the API, or leave it to us — depending on how involved you want to be.

04

You earn commission

For every paying client you receive a contractual commission, repeatedly for as long as their subscription lasts.

What you get from us

Recurring commission

This is not a one-off signup bonus — the commission keeps coming for as long as the client stays active.

Free demo account

Full access so you can show a client the widgets and the administration live.

Sales materials

A deck, case studies and an automatically generated PDF demo of the widgets on your client's store.

Priority support

A direct line to our team during the rollout, with no waiting in the standard queue.

Technical help with the integration

We help with GTM, the API and unusual custom setups.

Joint promotion

We turn a successful rollout into a case study where your brand gets space too.
