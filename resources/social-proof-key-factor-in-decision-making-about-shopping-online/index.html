Social Proof - a key factor in online purchase decisions

Our way of shopping has changed thanks to the internet. Online shopping has become a normal part of our lives. Customers are no longer limited to physical stores in their area. The internet allows them to choose and shop from a wide range of products and services from all over the world. However, with this influx of options and information comes new challenges. So how do customers choose between so many options? One of the key factors influencing the decision-making process of online shoppers is so-called 'social proof'.

What is Social Proof?

Social proof is a psychological phenomenon that is based on the human tendency to rely on the experiences and opinions of others to make decisions. In the context of online shopping, this means that customers seek confirmation of the quality and trustworthiness of a product or service from other people.

At first glance, Social Proof may sound simple - it's product reviews and ratings from other customers. However, this phenomenon involves much more complex aspects of human psychology and interactions.

Social proof is based on the tendency of people to make decisions based on what others do. It is a way of trying to mitigate the risk of decisions that may have unknown consequences. Why do we do this? Mostly because humans are social creatures and we expect other people to have better information or experiences that can help us.

Credibility and Social Proof

What makes Social Proof such a powerful tool? Credibility. When we see that a product has a lot of positive ratings and reviews, we feel more confident that this product will be good for us too. It's like getting a recommendation from someone we know, even if we've never met that person.

This recommendation comes in the form of a virtual voice. What other customers say gains weight because it appears to be an independent perspective. It's as if we have a team of "referrers" on our side telling us we're on the right track. This need for trust is especially significant in an online environment where we don't have the opportunity to see and feel products in person.

The Importance of Social Proof in Online Shopping

As online shopping does not allow customers to physically see, feel or try products before buying, relying on the experience of others is essential. Social proof provides customers with the reassurance that their decisions are not just based on marketing messages and product descriptions.

Different Forms of Social Proof

Social proof is not only manifested through reviews. It takes many forms. On the one hand, we have the number of units sold. If something has been sold thousands of times, it seems to be a product that is worthwhile. On the other hand, we have influencers and personalities who shape our decisions with their attitude and recommendations. This is also a form of social proof.

  1. Product Reviews and Ratings: one of the main ways that social proof manifests itself is through product reviews and ratings from actual customers. These feedbacks are often posted directly on vendors' websites or on third-party platforms. Customers are trying to find out what other people are saying about a product they are interested in.
  2. Number of Pieces Sold: Another indicator of social proof can be the number of units sold. If a product is popular and has sold many units, customers often see this number as an indicator of its reliability and popularity.
  3. Social Media and Influencers: Social media also plays an important role in social proof. Celebrities and influencers, who have a large following, can influence customers' decision-making process by recommending a certain product or sharing their own experiences.
  4. Referrals from Friends and Family: Customers often turn to their friends and family to learn about their experiences with certain products. These personal recommendations have a significant impact on decisions.

Implications For Online Shops

For online retailers, social proof is essential. Products and services with positive reviews and a high number of units sold are more likely to gain the trust of customers. Conversely, negative reviews and low interest can cause customers to opt for a different product or retailer.

Social Proof and the Future of Online Shopping

Given the growing trend of online shopping, the impact of social proof is expected to increase further. Customers will increasingly turn to reviews and ratings when making purchasing decisions. Therefore, it will be important for retailers to proactively manage their reputation and work towards gaining positive customer experiences.

Social proof is a tool that influences how customers choose and buy products online. Its powerful influence on the decision-making process is an essential part of today's e-commerce environment. It's a way for customers to feel more confident in their decisions, and it's an opportunity for retailers to present their products in their best yet truest light.
