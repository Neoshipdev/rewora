Smart search for your e-shop: find out what customers look for, what they cannot find and why they do not buy

Andrea Vargová23 November 2026

A customer is looking for a product you have in stock. They type its name into your search box, but your e-shop returns no results. They leave for a competitor. All it would have taken is for the search to understand another name for the same product.

The new smart product search from Rewora

But what if customers repeatedly search for products you do not carry at all? Or they do find the products and still do not buy them?

These are exactly the situations the new smart product search from Rewora helps to solve. It makes your range easier to navigate thanks to synonyms, autosuggestions and advanced filtering. At the same time it gives merchants statistics that reveal what visitors search for most often, which products they cannot find, and which searches end in low conversion.

The search box turns from a simple feature into a practical tool for optimising your product range, improving the shopping experience and spotting new sales opportunities.

What is smart product search on an e-shop?

Smart search is a feature that helps customers find relevant products even when they do not know the exact product name or use a different term from the one in your catalogue.

Customers often have no idea what the manufacturer calls a particular product. They only remember the brand, the colour, a certain feature or the everyday name for it.

Unlike basic search, which mainly relies on matching the entered words against product data, Rewora also lets you work with your own synonyms for products and categories.

Rewora smart search offers:

  • Search across products, categories and brands
  • Autosuggested results as the customer types
  • Custom synonyms for products and categories
  • Automatic inheritance of category synonyms by products
  • Result filtering by attributes, price and availability
  • Product cards with photos, prices, ratings and stock availability
  • Statistics on the most searched terms and products
  • An overview of searches with no results
  • Identification of products with hardly any searches
  • Analysis of searches with low conversion

The goal is not only to shorten the customer's path to the product, but also to help the merchant better understand customer demand.

Search with synonyms: when customers use different words from your e-shop

Imagine you sell electric scooters. In your product catalogue they are listed as "electric scooters", but the customer types "e-scooter" into the search box.

Or you sell sofa sets while the visitor is looking for a "couch".

With basic search the result can be an empty page, even though you have the product they want.

Rewora lets you assign synonyms to any product or category — additional terms under which the product should be findable.

How synonyms work in practice

Product name in the e-shop Synonyms you can add
Sofa set couch, settee, sectional
Mobile phone cell phone, smartphone, mobile
Electric scooter e-scooter, escooter
Running shoes running trainers, trainers for running
Cordless drill battery drill, cordless screwdriver
Refrigerator fridge, icebox

And you do not have to set synonyms product by product.

Category synonyms can pass automatically to products

One of Rewora's practical features is inheritance of synonyms from categories by individual products.

If you assign the synonym "couches" to the "Sofa sets" category, for example, the products in that category can automatically become findable under that term as well.

So there is no need to edit dozens or hundreds of products one at a time.

And if you do not want a particular product to use its category synonyms, you can switch the inheritance off.

Why synonyms matter for sales

Customers rarely use the manufacturer's technical terminology. They search the way they speak in everyday life.

Well-chosen synonyms therefore help to:

  • Reduce the number of unsuccessful searches.
  • Show relevant products even for alternative names.
  • Improve the findability of your range without changing official product names.
  • Make large product catalogues easier to navigate.
  • Limit the cases where a customer leaves simply because they could not find the product.

Synonyms are just as useful for regional terms, colloquial names or the English product names people have grown used to.

Autosuggested products as the customer types

Customers do not have to finish the whole search term or work through a long list of categories.

Rewora smart search shows suggested results while the text is still being typed, which shortens the path to the product the customer wants.

The search works with three types of content:

  • Products: specific items from the catalogue.
  • Categories: groups of products related to the search term.
  • Brands: manufacturers and brands matching the search.

If the customer starts typing a brand name, they can reach its products faster. If they enter the name of a product category, the search helps them carry on in the right direction.

This kind of search makes navigation easier above all in e-shops with an extensive range.

Clear search results with prices, ratings and availability

Finding the product is only the first step. The customer also needs to know whether it suits them and whether it is available.

That is why Rewora offers a dedicated search results page where products appear on clear cards.

On each card the customer sees:

  • A product photo.
  • A short description.
  • The product rating.
  • The current price.
  • Any discount.
  • Product availability.

Smart filtering of results

If the search returns a larger number of products, the customer can narrow them down further with filters.

Filters are available by:

  • product attributes and parameters
  • availability
  • price range.

The selected filters appear as separate tags the customer can easily remove.

Results also support pagination, so customers can move comfortably through larger sets of products.

Besides individual products, the results can also show matching categories and brands.

What do customers search for but never find? Search statistics reveal new sales opportunities

Do you know which products customers search for most often on your e-shop? And how many times they leave without finding what they need?

Every search is valuable information about customer demand. A visitor who types a specific term into the search box is already showing interest in a particular product, brand or category.

The new Rewora smart search is therefore not just a tool for finding products. It also gives merchants statistics that help them understand shopping behaviour, spot gaps in the range and uncover opportunities to increase sales.

In the Rewora administration you get an overview of four important areas:

  • Most searched terms and products – find out what customers are most interested in.
  • Terms with no results – reveal the products customers look for but cannot find.
  • Products with hardly any searches – identify the range customers show little interest in through search.
  • Searches with low conversion – find out which searches rarely turn interest into a purchase.

Most searched terms and products: find out what customers really want

Which products do customers actively search for on your e-shop? Which brands interest them? And what terms do they use?

Statistics on the most searched terms and products help you understand current customer demand.

You can use this information when planning stock levels, setting up marketing campaigns or deciding which products to highlight on the homepage.

If customers often search for a particular type of running shoes, for instance, you can check availability, extend the range of sizes or prepare a themed campaign.

Instead of deciding purely from past sales, you gain another view of what customers are actively looking for.

Terms with no results: reveal the products missing from your range

One of the most valuable statistics is the list of search terms that returned no results at all.

Imagine an e-shop selling coffee machines where customers repeatedly search for "cleaning tablets for coffee machines". The shop does not sell them yet.

In the Rewora statistics you can identify these unsuccessful searches and see what kind of goods customers are interested in.

For the e-shop this can be the impulse to extend the range with products that visitors already want.

Not every search without results means the product is missing, though.

Sometimes the customer searches for "couch" while the e-shop lists its products as "sofa sets". That is exactly where adding synonyms helps.

Search statistics and synonym settings therefore work together as a practical tool for optimising the product catalogue.

Terms with no results help you to:

  • Identify products and brands customers ask for but you do not stock.
  • Spot opportunities to extend the range.
  • Find missing synonyms and alternative product names.
  • Improve product names and descriptions.
  • Prevent cases where a customer leaves for a competitor just because they could not find the right product.

Products with hardly any searches: which part of the range goes unnoticed?

Some products have a good price, solid reviews and plenty of stock, and customers still barely search for them.

Rewora statistics help you identify products with hardly any searches.

Such products are not automatically uninteresting. They may be a less well-known part of the range, products with an unsuitable name, or items customers tend to discover through categories rather than the search box.

The merchant gains a basis for further analysis and can check whether product names need changing, whether synonyms should be added or whether the products need more visibility in marketing.

Searches with low conversion: customers search but do not buy

A customer types a specific product into the search box, the results appear, and no purchase follows.

If the same thing keeps happening, it can be a signal that customers are interested but something is stopping them from completing the order.

Rewora therefore also provides statistics on searches with low conversion.

Imagine, for example, that customers often search for "wireless headphones". The e-shop shows them relevant products, but only a small percentage of those searches leads to an order.

There can be several reasons:

  • A price too high compared with customer expectations.
  • The requested models being out of stock.
  • Too narrow a product selection.
  • Incomplete product information.
  • Weak ratings or a lack of reviews.
  • Results that do not match what the customer had in mind.

Low conversion on its own does not pinpoint the cause, but it flags an area worth checking.

The merchant can then gradually optimise the product range, prices, availability and product presentation.

How to use search statistics to increase sales

The greatest benefit of Rewora statistics is the ability to turn customer searches into concrete business decisions.

What the statistics reveal How the e-shop can respond
Customers often search for a specific product Highlight it, support it with ads or check stock levels
They repeatedly search for a product with no results Add the missing goods or set up synonyms
Some products get hardly any searches Review their naming, visibility and marketing support
Certain searches have low conversion Analyse prices, availability, reviews and result relevance

Rewora thus helps answer not only "What are customers buying?" but also "What do they want to buy and have not managed to yet?"

That information can be the key to a better-tuned product range, more effective marketing and more orders.

Better control over which products appear in search

Not every product in the catalogue has to be suitable for the search results.

Rewora therefore lets you manage the searchability of individual products.

The product administration includes a Catalog Search section where you can set:

  • Searchable: whether the product is included in search.
  • Synonyms: the additional terms under which the product should be findable.
  • Include category synonyms: whether the product inherits the synonyms of its categories.

The system also warns you if a product marked as searchable is missing the data needed to appear in search results.

In the product list you can filter the items search cannot display.

This is useful above all for larger e-shops where products are imported or updated regularly and not all of them end up with correctly filled-in data.

Managing synonyms through the administration, the API and AI tools

For e-shops with thousands of products, managing synonyms by hand can be time-consuming.

Rewora therefore offers several ways to set them.

Directly in the administration

Synonyms can be added and edited on individual products and categories. This approach suits ongoing catalogue maintenance.

Through the API

Products and categories can also be managed through the API, which opens up automation and integration with other systems.

Through MCP

Rewora also supports synonym management through MCP (Model Context Protocol), which connects it to compatible AI tools and agents.

That creates room for more efficient work with an extensive catalogue and its continuous optimisation.

Why good search matters for e-shop conversions

Search is not merely a technical feature. It is part of the customer journey.

A visitor who knows what they want expects to find the product quickly and without complications.

If they have to try different terms over and over, browse categories or manually filter hundreds of products, their shopping experience gets worse.

Smart search can help to:

  • Shorten the time needed to find a product.
  • Reduce the number of searches without a relevant result.
  • Make products easier to compare.
  • Increase the visibility of relevant goods.
  • Limit the departures caused by an unsuccessful search.
  • Support the conversion rate and sales.

A significant advantage of Rewora is that alongside the search itself it provides statistics that help uncover problem areas.

If certain searches show low conversion, for instance, the merchant can check whether customers are finding suitable products, whether those products are available and whether their price matches expectations.

Good search can therefore not only make buying easier, but also help identify the obstacles that limit an e-shop's sales potential.

Smart search as a source of insight for marketing and SEO

Search on an e-shop can be an interesting source of information about the language customers use.

Merchants often name products after manufacturers, technical parameters or internal categories. Customers, however, use simpler, colloquial or alternative names.

These findings can be used not only for synonyms, but also for:

  • Optimising product names and descriptions.
  • Creating new product categories.
  • Preparing articles and buying guides.
  • Choosing topics for content marketing.
  • Planning advertising campaigns.
  • Extending the range according to customer demand.

If customers regularly search for "a small coffee machine for the office", for example, that can be the impulse to create a buying guide or a relevant product collection.

Search statistics can therefore help with e-shop SEO as well. They reveal the terms customers really use and provide the basis for content that matches their needs.

Adding a synonym on its own will not improve a page's position in Google, though. The terms you discover also need to be worked properly into the content of the e-shop.

Who is Rewora smart search for?

Smart search can benefit smaller and larger e-shops alike, especially those with a wide range or products that go by several common names.

It matters, for example, for:

  • Electronics e-shops: customers use technical, English and colloquial names.
  • Furniture retailers: one type of product can have several everyday names.
  • Fashion e-shops: customers search by colour, type or features.
  • Tool shops: technical names often differ from the words DIY enthusiasts use.
  • Car parts e-shops: products may be labelled with several technical and commercial names.
  • Cosmetics retailers: customers may search by brand, type or specific properties.

The larger the catalogue and the more varied the product names, the more important it is to help customers reach relevant results.

And the more customers use the search, the more interesting the information an e-shop can gather about their shopping needs.

Frequently asked questions about smart search on an e-shop

What is smart search on an e-shop?

Smart search helps customers find products, categories and brands even when they use alternative names. Rewora supports synonyms, autosuggested results and product filtering by attributes, price and availability. It also provides search statistics that help merchants better understand customer demand.

What are synonyms in product search?

Synonyms are alternative names or terms under which a product should be findable. For the product "sofa set", for example, you can add the synonyms "couch", "settee" or "sectional".

Can I add synonyms to a whole product category?

Yes. Rewora lets you assign synonyms to categories and pass them automatically to the products in those categories. On individual products this inheritance can be switched off.

Can Rewora search brands and categories too?

Yes. The search works with products, categories and brands, and offers suggestions as soon as the customer starts typing.

How do I find out what customers search for most often on my e-shop?

Rewora provides statistics on the most searched terms and products. The e-shop can identify the products customers are most interested in and use that information when planning stock, marketing campaigns and the product range.

How do I find out which products customers could not find?

The Rewora smart search statistics include terms with no results. They show what customers searched for while the search returned no product at all. With this data you can identify missing goods or add synonyms to existing products.

Can search tell me which products are worth adding to the e-shop?

Yes. Repeatedly searched terms with no results can indicate unmet customer demand. Rewora statistics are therefore a useful basis for deciding how to extend the product range.

How do I find out why customers do not buy after searching?

Rewora lets you identify searches with low conversion. The merchant can then check the relevance of the results, prices, stock availability, the quality of product information and customer reviews, and look for the reasons behind the low number of orders.

Can synonyms be managed automatically?

Yes. Rewora supports synonym management through the administration, the API and MCP, which allows them to be built into automated workflows.

Does smart search help increase the conversion rate?

It can. When customers find relevant products more easily and can compare them faster, some of the obstacles to buying disappear. Search statistics also help identify terms with low conversion and reveal room for further optimisation.

Turn search into a better shopping experience and a source of business opportunities

With it you can find out not only what customers search for most often, but also which products they cannot find, which part of the range attracts little interest and which searches end in low conversion.

You can use this information to extend your range, improve product data, sharpen marketing campaigns and optimise the customer journey.

Because every unsuccessful search can be a sales opportunity your e-shop is not using yet.
