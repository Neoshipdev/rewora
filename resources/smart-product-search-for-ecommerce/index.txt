<h2>What is smart product search on an e-shop?</h2>
<p>Smart search is a feature that helps customers find relevant products even when they do not know the exact product name or use a different term from the one in your catalogue.</p>
<p>Customers often have no idea what the manufacturer calls a particular product. They only remember the brand, the colour, a certain feature or the everyday name for it.</p>
<p>Unlike basic search, which mainly relies on matching the entered words against product data, Rewora also lets you work with your own synonyms for products and categories.</p>
<p><strong>Rewora smart search offers:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Search across products, categories and brands</li>
<li>Autosuggested results as the customer types</li>
<li>Custom synonyms for products and categories</li>
<li>Automatic inheritance of category synonyms by products</li>
<li>Result filtering by attributes, price and availability</li>
<li>Product cards with photos, prices, ratings and stock availability</li>
<li>Statistics on the most searched terms and products</li>
<li>An overview of searches with no results</li>
<li>Identification of products with hardly any searches</li>
<li>Analysis of searches with low conversion</li>
</ul>
<p>The goal is not only to shorten the customer&#39;s path to the product, but also to help the merchant better understand customer demand.</p>

<p>Or you sell sofa sets while the visitor is looking for a &quot;couch&quot;.</p>
<p>With basic search the result can be an empty page, even though you have the product they want.</p>
<p><strong>Rewora lets you assign synonyms to any product or category</strong> — additional terms under which the product should be findable.</p>

<h3>Category synonyms can pass automatically to products</h3>
<p>One of Rewora&#39;s practical features is <strong>inheritance of synonyms from categories by individual products</strong>.</p>
<p>If you assign the synonym &quot;couches&quot; to the &quot;Sofa sets&quot; category, for example, the products in that category can automatically become findable under that term as well.</p>
<p>So there is no need to edit dozens or hundreds of products one at a time.</p>
<p>And if you do not want a particular product to use its category synonyms, you can switch the inheritance off.</p>
<h3>Why synonyms matter for sales</h3>
<p>Customers rarely use the manufacturer&#39;s technical terminology. They search the way they speak in everyday life.</p>
<p>Well-chosen synonyms therefore help to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Reduce the number of unsuccessful searches.</li>
<li>Show relevant products even for alternative names.</li>
<li>Improve the findability of your range without changing official product names.</li>
<li>Make large product catalogues easier to navigate.</li>
<li>Limit the cases where a customer leaves simply because they could not find the product.</li>
</ul>
<p>Synonyms are just as useful for regional terms, colloquial names or the English product names people have grown used to.</p>
<h2>Autosuggested products as the customer types</h2>
<p>Customers do not have to finish the whole search term or work through a long list of categories.</p>
<p>Rewora smart search shows <strong>suggested results while the text is still being typed</strong>, which shortens the path to the product the customer wants.</p>
<p>The search works with three types of content:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Products:</strong> specific items from the catalogue.</li>
<li><strong>Categories:</strong> groups of products related to the search term.</li>
<li><strong>Brands:</strong> manufacturers and brands matching the search.</li>
</ul>
<p>If the customer starts typing a brand name, they can reach its products faster. If they enter the name of a product category, the search helps them carry on in the right direction.</p>
<p>This kind of search makes navigation easier above all in e-shops with an extensive range.</p>

<p>Every search is valuable information about customer demand. A visitor who types a specific term into the search box is already showing interest in a particular product, brand or category.</p>

<li><strong>Products with hardly any searches</strong> – identify the range customers show little interest in through search.</li>

<p>Which products do customers actively search for on your e-shop? Which brands interest them? And what terms do they use?</p>

<p><strong>Instead of deciding purely from past sales, you gain another view of what customers are actively looking for.</strong></p>
<h3>Terms with no results: reveal the products missing from your range</h3>
<p>One of the most valuable statistics is the list of <strong>search terms that returned no results at all</strong>.</p>
<p>Imagine an e-shop selling coffee machines where customers repeatedly search for &quot;cleaning tablets for coffee machines&quot;. The shop does not sell them yet.</p>
<p>In the Rewora statistics you can identify these unsuccessful searches and see what kind of goods customers are interested in.</p>
<p>For the e-shop this can be the impulse to extend the range with products that visitors already want.</p>

<p>Sometimes the customer searches for &quot;couch&quot; while the e-shop lists its products as &quot;sofa sets&quot;. That is exactly where adding synonyms helps.</p>
<p><strong>Search statistics and synonym settings therefore work together as a practical tool for optimising the product catalogue.</strong></p>

<p>The merchant gains a basis for further analysis and can check whether product names need changing, whether synonyms should be added or whether the products need more visibility in marketing.</p>
<h3>Searches with low conversion: customers search but do not buy</h3>
<p>A customer types a specific product into the search box, the results appear, and no purchase follows.</p>
<p>If the same thing keeps happening, it can be a signal that customers are interested but something is stopping them from completing the order.</p>
<p><strong>Rewora therefore also provides statistics on searches with low conversion.</strong></p>
<p>Imagine, for example, that customers often search for &quot;wireless headphones&quot;. The e-shop shows them relevant products, but only a small percentage of those searches leads to an order.</p>
<p>There can be several reasons:</p>
<ul>
<li>A price too high compared with customer expectations.</li>
<li>The requested models being out of stock.</li>
<li>Too narrow a product selection.</li>
<li>Incomplete product information.</li>
<li>Weak ratings or a lack of reviews.</li>
<li>Results that do not match what the customer had in mind.</li>
</ul>
<p>Low conversion on its own does not pinpoint the cause, but it flags an area worth checking.</p>
<p>The merchant can then gradually optimise the product range, prices, availability and product presentation.</p>

<li>Reduce the number of searches without a relevant result.</li>

<p><strong>Good search can therefore not only make buying easier, but also help identify the obstacles that limit an e-shop&#39;s sales potential.</strong></p>

<ul>

<li>Creating new product categories.</li>
<li>Preparing articles and buying guides.</li>
<li>Choosing topics for content marketing.</li>

<p><strong>Search statistics can therefore help with e-shop SEO as well.</strong> They reveal the terms customers really use and provide the basis for content that matches their needs.</p>
<p>Adding a synonym on its own will not improve a page&#39;s position in Google, though. The terms you discover also need to be worked properly into the content of the e-shop.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions about smart search on an e-shop</h2>
<h3>What is smart search on an e-shop?</h3>
<p>Smart search helps customers find products, categories and brands even when they use alternative names. Rewora supports synonyms, autosuggested results and product filtering by attributes, price and availability. It also provides search statistics that help merchants better understand customer demand.</p>
<h3>What are synonyms in product search?</h3>
<p>Synonyms are alternative names or terms under which a product should be findable. For the product &quot;sofa set&quot;, for example, you can add the synonyms &quot;couch&quot;, &quot;settee&quot; or &quot;sectional&quot;.</p>
<h3>Can I add synonyms to a whole product category?</h3>
<p>Yes. Rewora lets you assign synonyms to categories and pass them automatically to the products in those categories. On individual products this inheritance can be switched off.</p>
<h3>Can Rewora search brands and categories too?</h3>
<p>Yes. The search works with products, categories and brands, and offers suggestions as soon as the customer starts typing.</p>
<h3>How do I find out what customers search for most often on my e-shop?</h3>
<p>Rewora provides statistics on the most searched terms and products. The e-shop can identify the products customers are most interested in and use that information when planning stock, marketing campaigns and the product range.</p>
<h3>How do I find out which products customers could not find?</h3>
<p>The Rewora smart search statistics include terms with no results. They show what customers searched for while the search returned no product at all. With this data you can identify missing goods or add synonyms to existing products.</p>
<h3>Can search tell me which products are worth adding to the e-shop?</h3>
<p>Yes. Repeatedly searched terms with no results can indicate unmet customer demand. Rewora statistics are therefore a useful basis for deciding how to extend the product range.</p>
<h3>How do I find out why customers do not buy after searching?</h3>
<p>Rewora lets you identify searches with low conversion. The merchant can then check the relevance of the results, prices, stock availability, the quality of product information and customer reviews, and look for the reasons behind the low number of orders.</p>
<h3>Can synonyms be managed automatically?</h3>
<p>Yes. Rewora supports synonym management through the administration, the API and MCP, which allows them to be built into automated workflows.</p>
<h3>Does smart search help increase the conversion rate?</h3>
<p>It can. When customers find relevant products more easily and can compare them faster, some of the obstacles to buying disappear. Search statistics also help identify terms with low conversion and reveal room for further optimisation.</p>

<p>It can help customers find the right goods, make decisions easier and at the same time give the merchant valuable insight into customer needs.</p>
