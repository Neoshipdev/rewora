c:Ta29,<p>Startup Rewora, which is changing the way e-shops work with customer trust, is the winner of the Upsteer by Asseco CE incubation program. After six months of intensive work, product validation and growth within the program, Rewora was awarded the top spot among nine finalists.</p>
<p><img src="/rewora/images/media/DSC09129-edit.format-avif.width-768.avif" alt=""></p>
<p>Since 2023, Rewora has been helping e-shops across Europe increase conversion and customer trust with advanced tools to collect, manage and leverage reviews. The platform enables e-shops to collect reviews at key points in the customer journey, display them on the web, improve transparency and offers <strong>Business Intelligence, a helpdesk/forum, hot spots, Google Shopping integration and automatic translations</strong>. Products with at least five reviews are up to <strong>270% more likely to sell</strong>, and it is Rewora that is helping e-tailers actively take advantage of this opportunity.</p>
<p>The startup isn&#39;t just going the classic review software route - it&#39;s also building <strong>TrustGraph™</strong>, a technology that creates personalized &quot;trust influencers&quot; based on customer behavior, recommendations and influence. This way, each customer sees content ranked in a way that maximizes the likelihood of purchase for them, leading to a dramatic increase in both conversion and organic traffic.</p>
<p><strong>&quot;Winning Upsteer is a huge validation for us that we are solving a real problem in European e-commerce. Thank you to the organisers of Upsteer by Asseco, all the mentors and especially to the entire Rewora team for an extremely intense six months,&quot;</strong> said the Rewora team.</p>
<p>Rewora is already generating revenue, growing in both pilots and paying domains, and plans to expand into foreign markets. Winning the incubator opens up new opportunities for the startup in both local and international markets.</p>
