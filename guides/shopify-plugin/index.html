Installing the Rewora Product Reviews app on Shopify

From installing it in the Shopify App Store, through activating the widget in your theme, to approving the first review. You can do the whole rollout yourself, without touching the theme code.

01

Installing the app

  1. In the Shopify admin go to Apps → Shopify App Store (or straight to apps.shopify.com).
  2. Search for rewora and open Rewora Product Reviews ($4.99/month, 7-day free trial).
  3. Click Install.
  4. An Install app window appears listing the permissions the app needs. Confirm with Install.
02

Choosing and approving the plan

  1. The Select a plan screen opens with a Pay monthly / Pay yearly switch. The Standard plan costs $4.99 / 30 days or $49.99 / year ($9 cheaper), with 7 days free. Click Select.
  2. On the Approve charge screen check the plan and click Approve.
    • If Shopify says “You don’t have any payment methods on file”, add a payment method first via Go to billing settings — otherwise Approve cannot be completed.
03

Activating the app embed in your theme

  1. After installation the app's start page opens: Rewora → Reviews.
  2. In the 1. Activate app embed block click Activate App Embed. The theme editor opens with the App embeds panel.
  3. Switch Rewora – Rewora Product Reviews on and click Save in the top right.
04

Adding the widget to the product page

  1. Go back to the app and in the 2. Add widget to your page block click Add to the page.
  2. A Product Reviews block is added to the Default product template in the theme editor. Drag it wherever you want it.
  3. In the left panel set the widget's look so it matches your brand:
    • Main colors – Text color, Text small color, Text caption color, Background color, Border color
    • Action – Text on action color, Action color, Action hover color (the Write a review button)
    • Input – the form field colours
    • Feedback colors – Positive / Negative color and their backgrounds
    • Other – Stars color, Empty stars color, Delete icon color, Modal background, Avatar background
  4. Click Save. A Reviews and Ratings section with 1–5 star filters appears on the product page.
05

How a customer adds a review

On the product page they click Write a review and fill in the form:

  • Overall rating – stars, required
  • Review title – max. 150 characters
  • Review – min. 5 characters
  • Positives & Negatives – any number of pros and cons
  • Images & Videos – up to 5 images (JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, max. 10 MB) and a video (MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WEBM, max. 40 MB)
  • Your name – left empty, the review stays anonymous
  • Your email – required, never published, used only for verification
  • They tick the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy consent and hit Send

Customers can also be asked for a review automatically by e-mail after a purchase — the number of days after the order and the sender address are set in Settings in the app. The same place lets you turn on review imports from Heureka.sk, Heureka.cz and Arukereso (an account key is required).

06

Approving a review

  1. In the Shopify admin go to Apps → Rewora Product Reviews → Reviews Management.
  2. The list has All / Not approved / Approved tabs and the columns Title, Product name, Review text, Rating source, Rating, Is approved, Reviewed at. A new review has Is approved = No and is not visible in the store yet.
  3. There are two ways to approve it:
    • In bulk: tick the checkbox next to the review → Approve / Reject appears above the list → click Approve.
    • From the detail: click the review title. You see the Summary text, Pros, Cons and an Info panel (product, rating, source, date) with an approve/reject button.
  4. Once approved the column switches to Is approved = Yes, the detail gains an Approved at timestamp and the button turns into a red Reject (approval can be undone at any time).
  5. Check the result in the store — the product page now shows the average rating (e.g. 5.0, 1 review), the 1–5 star breakdown, the author's name, the date, the text, pros and cons, any attached photo and Was this helpful? / Report.
What to watch out for

The widget will not show if the app embed is off, or if you did not click Save in the theme editor after adding the block. And a review only appears in the store once approved — until then it stays in Reviews Management.
