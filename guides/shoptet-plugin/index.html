Installing the Rewora add-on on Shoptet

You install the add-on straight from the Shoptet Doplňky catalogue — without touching your template. This guide takes you from installation through activating the subscription to approving the first review.

01

Installing the app

  1. In the Shoptet administration go to Connections → Shoptet Doplňky.
  2. Search for rewora and open the Rewora add-on.
  3. The right-hand column shows “Free to install” under Your price — click Order and install.
  4. Confirm the add-on order. Shoptet takes you back to the administration, where Rewora now appears among your connections.
02

Verifying your e-mail and opening the admin

  1. In Connections → Shoptet Doplňky open Rewora — the “Finish setting up your store” screen appears.
  2. Verify your e-mail address. We sent the confirmation link to the store's contact e-mail.
    • If the e-mail did not arrive, use Resend verification e-mail and check your spam folder.
  3. Once verified, the Open Rewora administration button becomes active and takes you to your account.
03

Activating the subscription

  1. In the Rewora admin open the Billing section.
  2. Use the Monthly / Yearly switch to pick the period and choose a plan — Standard at €29 or Professional at €149 per month. Click Subscribe.
  3. Payment runs through Stripe: enter your card details or choose Revolut Pay and confirm Pay and subscribe. VAT is added according to your country.
  4. The subscription is risk-free — if you are not satisfied, we refund your money within 30 days of purchase.
04

Configuring the store

  1. In Settings check the store name and domain URL, upload your logo and, if you use it, the Business Intelligence data file.
  2. Add the URL of your terms and conditions and the language code. If you sell in several countries, turn on automatic translation for the widgets and pick the languages.
  3. Set when the review request should be sent:
    • a short-term and a long-term request after delivery (for example 7 and 70 days),
    • a short-term and a long-term request after purchase when the delivery date is unknown (for example 21 and 90 days).
  4. The right-hand column holds the URL patterns of your store (product, catalogue, cart, checkout, successful order, homepage) and the “Add to cart” button selector. With a standard Shoptet template you do not need to change them.
  5. Save the settings.
05

How it looks in the store

Once activated, the widgets appear without any template changes. The product page gains:

  • a star rating and review count right next to the product name,
  • a Reviews and ratings section with the average and the 1–5 star breakdown,
  • a Write a review button with a form — rating, title, text, pros and cons,
  • the option to attach photos (JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP up to 10 MB) or a video.

You manage which widgets are shown in the Widgets section of the Rewora admin.

06

Approving a review

  1. In the Rewora admin open Reviews → Reviews and click the new review.
  2. The detail shows the title, rating, text, pros and cons, the author, the review source and the language.
  3. Click Mark as approved in the top right — the review appears in the store straight away. The Featured toggle lets you highlight it above the others.
  4. You find the status of review requests for a given order in the Orders section — it shows whether the short-term or long-term request has already been sent.
What to watch out for

The widgets only appear in the store once your e-mail is verified and the subscription is active. A new review goes public only after approval — until then you see it just in the Rewora admin.
