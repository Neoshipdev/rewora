# How we increased sales by over 10% for FixServis using reviews

> Zdroj: https://rewora.com/case-studies/how-are-we-for-fix-service-using-reviews-to-increase-turnover-by-10/
> Stiahnuté: 2026-08-26





FixServis was one of the e-shops that wanted to fully leverage the potential of reviews to increase sales, boost trust and perform better in Google Shopping. By deploying Rewora, they gained a consistent display of reviews across all countries, automated translations, fast feedback processing, and a continuous stream of authentic reviews. The result was a noticeable increase in both conversions and turnover in the first few months, with FixServis experiencing approximately 10% revenue growth, a stronger reputation, and higher visibility even in modern LLM models.

### **About the company**

iFix s.r.o., operating under the brand FixServis, is one of the strongest players on the Slovak market with spare parts and electronics service. It combines a high standard of expertise with fast availability of parts for smartphones, laptops, computers, vacuum cleaners, scooters and other modern technology. FixServis thus reaches a wide range of customers - from ordinary users to professional technicians and wholesale partners.

### **Results & Findings**

Rewora brought FixServis a rapid improvement in both customer experience and e-commerce performance. The regular influx of quality reviews has increased trust and encouraged faster decision making by customers who ask fewer questions before buying. The strongest effect was the automated translations and consistent display of reviews across countries, which boosted Google Shopping results and overall satisfaction. FixServis also saves significant time through automated moderation.

Today, FixServis appears as the **No. 1 recommended choice** in modern LLM models as well, giving it a new form of visibility and trust.
