# Overview of user content types

> Zdroj: https://rewora.com/resources/overview-of-user-content-types/
> Stiahnuté: 2026-08-26

**Náhľad:** /media/images/1690545009141.hd.webp



User Generated Content (UGC) is a valuable asset for every brand and business. When customers create brand-related content, they create authenticity, increase credibility, and engage with the community. In this article, we'll look at the different types of UGC that have huge potential for business.

1. Customer reviews. Authentic product reviews by other customers help potential customers make informed decisions and build trust in your brand.

2. Customer photos and videos: customers often share photos or videos of them using your products. Posting this type of content on your site adds a visual dimension and authenticity to your brand.

3. Create contests and challenges that encourage customers to create content related to your brand or products. This way you engage people and can create fun and engaging content.

4. Unboxings and reviews on social media.

5. User Stories. User stories are compelling and inspiring stories that encourage other customers to buy.

6. Tutorials and tips. These helpful hints will make customers benefit more from your product.

7. Influencer reviews.

8. Quizzes and voting. In this way, they get involved and actively contribute to the creation of content related to your brand.

9. Discussion forum and helpdesk. This type of UGC creates a community experience, takes away pre-purchase uncertainty and strengthens relationships with your customers.

Remember that an important part of using UGC effectively is encouraging customers and giving positive incentives for their active participation. Proper moderation and responding to content also plays a key role in maintaining a positive and constructive environment.

Try different types of UGC and watch your community grow, engage and strengthen customer relationships. User Generated Content is the path to authenticity, trust and success for your business.
