How to gain a competitive advantage from user-generated content

If you're still unfamiliar with the term "User Generated Content" (UGC), it's time to familiarize yourself with it.

UGC is a great way to engage your target audience, increase trust in your brand and boost the success of your marketing activities. In this article, we'll outline the reasons why you should start using UGC in your e-commerce business and how to start collecting UGC from your customers from the simplest forms like reviews to more challenging videos.

**1. Trust is the key to purchasing decisions**

The strength of the recommendation is undeniable. When potential customers see that other satisfied customers praise your products or services, they will have more trust in your brand. Reviews, ratings, and positive comments from real users can lead to **increased conversions** and **reduced return rates**.

**2. Creating an active community**

Using UGC allows you to create an engaged community around your brand. Customers feel that their opinions matter and that they are part of your company. You can encourage discussions, competitions and challenges that encourage people to share their experiences with your products.

**3. Personality and authenticity**

UGC brings a more human dimension to your brand. Real people sharing their stories, photos and videos create authentic content that **reaches your target audience more than traditional advertising**. UGC is immediate and authentic, which helps build a real relationship with customers.

**4. From reviews to videos - the diversity of UGC**

UGC acquisition can start simply with product reviews on your website or **review system**. You can then incentivize customers to share their photos with your products on social media and create content related to your brand.

To take it to the next level, user-generated videos are the most powerful form of UGC. Customers can share **unboxings**, **reviews**, product **tutorials**, or simply **interesting moments** they have related to your products.

**Collection and management of UGC:**

The most important step in UGC collection is to encourage customers to create content. You can motivate them with rewards, contests, or simply by personally sharing their content.

It's also important to have good **moderation practices** in place to minimize any negative content. As UGC is authentic, negative content may occur and you must deal with it in accordance with your terms and conditions and the legislation in force in the country where your customer is from.

Harness the potential of UGC in your e-commerce business and **start collecting reviews** and other forms of content from your customers. You'll see that authentic yet entertaining content created by your customers can have a **huge positive impact** on your store and create a loyal customer base.
