# Marketing strategy

> Zdroj: https://rewora.com/resources/marketing-strategy/
> Stiahnuté: 2026-09-04





Choosing the right marketing strategy for your brand can be challenging, because there are many options. In this article we look at the four main ones:

**1. Content marketing** is a strategy focused on creating quality content that is interesting for your target audience. Content can take many formats, such as blogs, videos or e-books. The goal of content marketing is to build trust in the brand and educate potential customers about your products or services. This strategy is ideal for businesses that want to improve their organic reach and build a brand that is seen as an authority in the field.

**2. Performance marketing** is aimed at achieving specific results through various marketing channels, such as PPC ads, affiliate marketing and email marketing. The goal is to increase conversions, acquire new customers and maximize profit. Performance marketing is ideal for businesses that want to see immediate results and analyze the success of their campaigns.

**3. Social media marketing** uses social media as a marketing channel. This strategy focuses on creating content that fits social media and engages your target audience. The goal of social media marketing is to raise brand awareness, engage the audience and build brand loyalty. This strategy is ideal for businesses that want to increase their visibility on social media and build a strong online presence.

**4. Influencer marketing** focuses on collaborating with influencers who have large followings on social media. The goal is to get more attention for your brand from their fans or followers. The main advantage of influencer marketing is that it lets businesses reach a specific, interested audience through the influencer’s trust and authenticity. Influencer marketing is ideal for businesses that want to raise brand awareness and reach new audiences through recommendations and proven ways of using their products or services.

Every marketing strategy has its **advantages and disadvantages**, and the right choice depends on your brand’s goals and your target audience. **Content marketing** and **social media marketing** focus on **building the brand and the relationship with the audience**, while **performance marketing** and **influencer marketing** focus on **fast results** and increasing conversions. It is important to understand what your goals are and which marketing channels best fit your needs.
