# How user-generated content (UGC) and artificial intelligence (AI) are taking online shopping to the next level

> Zdroj: https://rewora.com/resources/how-content-created-by-ugc-users-and-artificial-intelligence-does-shift-online-shopping-to-a-new-level/
> Stiahnuté: 2026-08-26

**Náhľad:** /media/images/robot-handshake-human-background-.hd.webp



In the era of digital transformation, customer expectations are constantly changing, and companies must adopt new approaches to ensure customer loyalty and competitive capabilities. In this new reality, the combination of User Generated Content (UGC) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) is emerging, bringing with it revolutionary capabilities that are changing the way brands communicate and interact with their customers.

**Authenticity builds credibility:**

One of the challenges of online shopping is the lack of tangible experiences. This is where UGC comes into play as an answer to this challenge. Today, 94% of online shoppers regularly read reviews and 77% look for photos and videos from other consumers before making a purchase. Customers trust authentic experiences of other customers more than company statements and product descriptions. UGC addresses the trust gap and provides valuable information that guides purchasing decisions.

User Generated Content is essentially the digital equivalent of word of mouth advertising or recommendations. It includes reviews, ratings, photos, videos, comments and discussions that real users share about products or services they have experienced. Unlike marketing campaigns, UGC is authentic, unfiltered and truthful. This authenticity forms its foundation.

But this is where AI comes in and changes the game. With powerful algorithms to analyze large amounts of data, AI can uncover hidden patterns and trends in user content. It analyzes UGC data and figures out what people like and dislike. Based on this, it helps improve products, tailoring them to the exact needs of customers. In this way, it solves a problem experienced by many consumers - finding the right product among many options.

These insights help brands better understand customer preferences and expectations, which in turn leads to better products and content tailored to customer needs.

**Personalized Recommendations:**

One of the main benefits of AI is the ability to make personalized recommendations. By analyzing user behavior and patterns in UGC, AI can guess what customers might be interested in. These recommendations become a valuable tool in customer acquisition and retention as they allow brands to communicate with customers in a relevant and effective way.

**Community Expansion:**

UGC is not just about reviews and ratings. It also includes discussions, questions and answers that form a community of customers. AI comes in here as a moderator and analyst. Through language processing and content analysis, AI can spot important topics, answer questions, and identify problems that customers are solving. In this way, it contributes to strengthening the community and building customer relationships.

**The Future is Here:**

It is clear that the future of online interactions is closely linked to UGC and AI. Brands that are ready to leverage these tools have the opportunity to build deeper and more meaningful relationships with customers. UGC adds authenticity and connects the community, while AI adds intelligence, deep analytics and personalization. Together, they are changing the way brands can approach their customers and opening the door to new levels of engagement, loyalty and personalization.
