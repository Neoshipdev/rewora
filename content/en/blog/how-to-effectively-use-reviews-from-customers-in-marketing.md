# How to use customer reviews effectively in marketing?

> Zdroj: https://rewora.com/resources/how-to-effectively-use-reviews-from-customers-in-marketing/
> Stiahnuté: 2026-08-26

**Náhľad:** /media/images/5401265.hd.webp



Customer reviews are a valuable source of feedback, but many companies don't realise that they are also a powerful marketing tool. They are a golden treasure that can take your marketing strategy to a whole new level. Why leave reviews and comments solely to Heureka or Google when they can impact almost every aspect of your marketing plan?

**Where can you use reviews everywhere?**

1. Website: reviews on your product page strengthen the credibility of your e-shop. They also add momentum to your content and increase its relevance to search engines.

2. Social networks: Adding reviews to social media posts will attract attention and increase user engagement. Real customer experiences have a much greater impact than traditional advertising.

3. Email marketing: including reviews in email campaigns increases their effectiveness. Customers trust other customers more than they trust you.

4. Blogging: This not only adds value to the content but also encourages interaction with customers.

**Impact on organic traffic:**

Reviews have an extraordinary impact on your website's organic traffic. Search engines are increasingly user-centric, and authentic reviews are among these valuable pieces of content. When your e-commerce store appears prominently in search engines thanks to quality reviews, you're gaining new customers before you even realize it.

**Increase credibility and conversion:**

Reviews bring an element of credibility to your marketing activities that is invaluable. Potential customers are more likely to rely on other consumers rather than your advertising.

Reviews also have a direct impact on the conversion rate. Positive experiences of other customers can persuade potential customers to buy.

**More than just text:**

You can use reviews in a variety of content formats. Why limit yourself to text-only reviews? Create video reviews, infographics, or stories of satisfied customers. This diverse content can attract a diverse group of customers.

**A key tool for post-purchase marketing:**

Using reviews in post-purchase marketing is a great way to keep customers closer to your online store. Personalized recommendation emails based on their previous purchases and reviews can increase sales from repeat customers. You can also leverage the power of discount coupons in post-purchase marketing as a form of thank you for a review, as well as a tool to gain additional conversions.
