About us

We are changing how online stores work with trust

Rewora is a Slovak SaaS platform for customer experience. We started with pilots; today we influence the revenue of dozens of stores in Slovakia and Czechia — and that is only the beginning.

From Slovakia we build technology for European e-commerce

We have been building Rewora since 2023. Behind the platform is a tight-knit team from technology, e-commerce, sales and marketing who have worked together for years and combine experience from Slovak and international technology companies.

Today Rewora helps online stores work with customer trust across more than 100 domains, and our ambitions reach far beyond Slovakia.

In 2025 we took first place in the Upsteer by Asseco CE incubation programme. We see it as confirmation that the problem we are solving is real — and that the way customers shop online can be done better.

We believe a brand's best salesperson is its customer.

Rewora grew out of a simple idea: people trust other people's experiences more than advertising. So we help stores turn their customers' experiences into trust — and trust into purchases.

We collect, connect and display authentic product and store reviews exactly where the customer needs them to decide.

Rewora is not just a tool for stars and ratings, though. We combine reviews, social proof, customer questions and answers, product data, Google Shopping, interactive content and analytics in one platform that helps customers decide more easily and stores sell more.

Why Rewora was founded

Europe is the second largest e-commerce market after Asia and eight in ten Europeans shop online. Even so, up to 85% of carts are abandoned — the customer is unsure about the product, the store, or both.

That mistrust costs online stores billions — while the remedy is within reach: their own customers' experiences. Reviews are more credible than advertising and weigh more in a decision than price.

That is why Rewora brings together everything you can do with social proof in one platform — product and store reviews, Q&A and a forum, hotspots, product BI data and rating transfer to Google Shopping.

Trust is a measurable asset

For us, customer trust is a measurable asset

We do not want reviews to be a mere add-on at the bottom of a product page. We want to turn them into one of the store's strongest sales tools.

Rewora works with customer experience across the whole buying journey — from collecting feedback automatically, through showing it at the products, to using reviews in search engines, Google Shopping and everywhere else the customer makes up their mind.
